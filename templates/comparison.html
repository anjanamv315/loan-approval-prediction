Academic Insights & Analytical Discussion
Why Accuracy Alone is Insufficient

The dataset features an imbalanced class distribution where 68.7% of applicants are approved (Y) and only 31.3% are rejected (N). A naive baseline predicting "Approved" for all candidates would attain 68.7% accuracy while completely failing to detect credit risk. Hence, F1-score and class-specific Precision and Recall are critical to penalize false approvals (credit defaults) and false rejections.

Model Performance Synthesis

Logistic Regression and Random Forest demonstrated superior recall on approved candidates (~99%), correctly identifying almost all creditworthy applicants. Random Forest demonstrated the strongest 5-fold cross-validation F1-score (0.8638) on the training set, benefiting from bootstrap aggregation (bagging) which dampens variance and individual decision tree instability.
